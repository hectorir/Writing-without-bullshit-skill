# Blog Post Playbook

Loaded for: blog post, article. Apply alongside 10 habits in `SKILL.md`.

Target: <750 words. Longer → use report or series.

## ROAM target sentence

Before drafting:

> *After reading this, [current and potential readers] will [learn something useful], so they will [share the post / subscribe / take a specific action] and think of me as [a helpful expert on this topic].*

Can't write it → post not ready. Long tier: ask for missing parts first.

## The hook does most of the work

Title + first sentences carry post. They show up in search results, link previews, email previews. Spend disproportionate time on title. Reader sees only title → does it communicate something useful?

- ✅ "Three numbers that explain why your team's velocity dropped"
- ❌ "Some thoughts on velocity"

First sentence rewards click. Front-load payoff. No "In this post, we'll explore…"

## Structural toolkit

Walls of paragraphs lose readers, esp. on phones. Use:

- **Subheads** as guideposts (max two levels)
- **Bullets with bolded openers** for skimmability
- **Numbered lists** when sequence or count matters
- **Tables** for parallel structured information
- **Pull quote** for the one line you want shared
- **Links** to sources and related posts

Reader skimming subheads + bullets → should get main message. If not → restructure.

## Graphics

One shareable graphic per post minimum. Plan with outline, not after. In draft, mark placement inline: `[Graphic: bar chart, velocity by quarter, caption states the drop]`. Caption states the takeaway, not the chart title.

## Ending

Close with the action from the ROAM target: subscribe, share, try the thing, read the related post. One action. No summary paragraph.

## Common failure modes

- **Buried lead.** Point shows up in para 6. Move to first sentence.
- **No call to action.** Readers finish + bounce.
- **Generic title.** "Thoughts on X." Earn it.
- **Hedged conclusion.** "It depends" rarely most useful. Take position.
- **Invented specifics.** Numbers, quotes, or customer names the user never supplied. Placeholder, not fabrication.
