# Weasel Words — Starter List

Low-info words + phrases. Scorecard, weasel flags, and self-check all scan this. Starting point, not closed set.

## Qualifiers

Sound like content but say nothing measurable. Replace with number, named subgroup, or bold claim. No number available → bracketed placeholder `[X%]`, never an invented figure.

## AI tells

Patterns that mark prose as machine-written. Reader who spots one discounts everything after it. Cut on sight.

- **Em-dash as glue.** Joins clauses that wanted a period or comma. Zero in email; one per longer piece at most.
- **"It's not X, it's Y."** Also "This isn't about X." Contrast frame that invents a straw man. State Y.
- **Forced triplets.** "Fast, reliable, and secure." Three because three sounds finished. Use the real count.
- **AI vocabulary.** delve, leverage (verb), seamless, robust, landscape, tapestry, navigate (a topic), unlock, empower, elevate, streamline, game-changer, journey, dive in, crucial, pivotal, testament to, underscore, foster, harness.
- **Flattering openers.** "Great question," "You're absolutely right," "What a thoughtful ask."
- **Announced structure.** "Here's the thing:", "Let's break it down:", "In this post, we'll explore…"
- **Bold label that echoes.** "**Speed:** we made it faster." Bolded opener adds; never restates.
- **Closing offers and sign-offs.** "Let me know if you'd like me to adjust," "Hope this helps!" Piece ends when content ends.
- **Summary paragraph.** "In conclusion, as we've seen…" Repeats the piece. Delete.

## Beyond the list

Also flag:

- Jargon non-expert reader wouldn't understand
- Buzzwords signaling in-group membership without meaning (e.g., "synergy," "operationalize")
- Empty intensifiers Claude judges context-specific filler
- Adjectives that state the conclusion prose should earn

In doubt → would sentence lose meaning without this word? If no → remove.
